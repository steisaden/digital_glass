# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective clients evaluating Stephen's design and development work, especially service businesses, operators, and e-commerce brands that need a conversion-focused website, AI follow-up system, or custom automation.

## Product Purpose

Stephen.Tech is a hybrid studio portfolio: it leads with tangible design and engineering work, then helps qualified visitors understand the services available and book a consultation. Success means a visitor can quickly judge the quality and range of the work, identify a relevant capability, and confidently start a conversation.

## Positioning

Stephen.Tech joins expressive, high-craft web design with the practical acquisition and operations systems behind it. The portfolio demonstrates both visual range and the ability to build lead generation, commerce, booking, AI follow-up, and automation infrastructure.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from direct links, referrals, GitHub, and project demos. They evaluate the site on desktop and mobile, browse live design demos and deeper case studies, compare service packages, and contact Stephen through the consultation flow or the published email and WhatsApp details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The product is a responsive Next.js portfolio and studio website.
- The portfolio is named Stephen.Tech.
- Portfolio projects may open as richer case studies or link to live external demonstrations.
- Services and pricing remain supporting conversion content rather than the first impression.
- Booking a consultation remains the primary conversion action.
- Existing case-study claims, pricing, and contact details are preserved unless separately verified or revised.
- Consultation actions route directly to the published email and WhatsApp details; the site does not simulate a submitted form.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the Stephen.Tech name.
- Preserve glassmorphism as a core material.
- Preserve the current Inter and Geist typography.
- Preserve a subtle shader background.
- The experience should feel authored and distinctive, avoiding generic AI-generated landing-page patterns.
- The voice is direct, technically credible, and outcome-oriented.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work before explaining the service.
2. Make every visual flourish support comprehension, trust, or project discovery.
3. Demonstrate range without making the studio identity feel fragmented.
4. Separate verified proof from illustrative or projected outcomes.
5. Keep the path from interest to consultation obvious and low-friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion preferences, readable contrast over animated backgrounds, semantic landmarks, and responsive layouts from small mobile screens through large desktops.
