## Scope and mode

Four reviewable homepage directions for Stephen.Tech. Visitor mode: Experience.

## Audience, job, and action

Prospective clients need to judge Stephen's visual range and technical credibility quickly. The primary action is to view a live project; the conversion action is to book a consultation.

## Proof and content

Lead with the five supplied live design demos, supported by the existing Goddess Care Co and Monumentaal build evidence. Services and pricing remain supporting content.

## Constraints

Every direction uses the same Stephen.Tech identity: Inter and Geist, dark optical glass, the warm Paper shader, real project captures, keyboard-accessible controls, responsive linearization, and reduced-motion support. Do not use a timed loader, fabricated proof, or simulated form delivery.

## Directions and memorable moments

- Orbit: projects gather around one selected artifact, which moves into focus.
- Showreel: scrolling advances through cinematic project acts and a slim chapter rail.
- Index: a keyboard-accessible project ledger drives a generous preview stage.
- Cabinet: an asymmetric field of translucent specimens expands the focused work.

Orbit is the recommended homepage baseline. Index is the recommended archive structure.

## Unresolved decision

The user will choose which direction becomes the production homepage after reviewing all four implemented concepts.
